01 / About

Clinical insight meets engineering design.

I am a final-year Biomedical Engineering (Honours) student at the University of Technology Sydney and currently work as a Biomedical Engineering Technician at Royal Prince Alfred Hospital. In this role, I work hands-on with medical equipment through servicing, fault diagnosis, preventative maintenance, testing and verification, while documenting equipment performance and working with biomedical engineering and clinical staff to help ensure devices are safe, reliable and available for patient care.

Alongside my clinical engineering work, I have experience in biotechnology research and medical-device development, with projects spanning diagnostics, embedded systems, electronics, CAD and prototyping. Working across clinical, research and design environments has shaped how I approach engineering problems, considering technical performance alongside safety, usability and the needs of clinicians and patients.

“I want to help medical-device teams build solutions that are technically sound, clinically useful and designed around the people who depend on them.”

To build my career in medical-device research and development, applying my clinical engineering experience and technical skills to design and develop innovative technologies that address real clinical and patient needs and improve the safety, reliability and accessibility of healthcare.

03 / Selected Projects

Engineering projects.

A focused selection of academic projects demonstrating medical-device design, diagnostics, embedded systems, biomechanics and research.

Point-of-care diagnostics · Capstone RESearch

MaterNix — Portable PCR for Group B Streptococcus

Developed key subsystems of a portable PCR platform for point-of-care Group B Streptococcus screening.

  • Translated clinical and stakeholder needs into measurable engineering requirements.
  • Developed thermal, sensing, power, enclosure and user-interface architecture.
  • Used design trade-offs and verification planning to guide iterative prototyping and improve performance and usability.
Point-of-care Diagnostics · Team leadership and Research

StrikeDx — Multiplex LFA for Stroke Differentiation

Led the development of a multiplex lateral-flow diagnostic concept for rapid differentiation of ischaemic and haemorrhagic stroke using GFAP and NT-proBNP biomarkers.

  • Contributed biomarker selection, assay architecture and quantitative analysis.
  • Modelled and 3D printed an ergonomic cassette in SolidWorks.
  • Built practical LFA workflow capability using ImageJ and MATLAB analysis.
Biomedical instrumentation · embedded systems

Wireless Pulse Oximetry & Temperature Telemetry

Developed an end-to-end physiological monitoring system integrating pulse oximetry and temperature sensing with embedded processing, wireless telemetry and real-time dashboard visualisation.

  • Integrated PPG and temperature sensing with a microcontroller.
  • Implemented filtering and feature extraction to improve measurement stability.
  • Benchmarked outputs against a commercial reference device.
Biomechanics · FEA · Additive manufacturing

3D-Printed Bone Scaffold for Osteoporotic Defects

Designed and evaluated a 3D-printed scaffold for osteoporotic bone defects, balancing porosity and mechanical performance to better replicate bone structure.

  • Created SolidWorks models and utilised FEA to assess stress, strain and mechanical integrity.
  • Performed compression, density and hardness testing.
  • Selected a 27% porosity circular-strut design using simulation and experimental evidence.
Biomechanical design · Optimisation

Functionally Graded Spinal Cage

Developed and optimised a functionally graded porous lumbar fusion cage to improve load distribution, support bone integration and reduce stress concentration at the vertebral endplate.

  • Used parametric CAD and FEA to compare design variants.
  • Optimised porosity, architecture and lordotic geometry.
  • Prepared and iterated a 3D-printable prototype for manufacturability.
Research · Nanomedicine

Nanoparticle-Based Lung Cancer Cell Imaging

Investigated antibody-conjugated nanoparticles for selective lung-cancer cell imaging and quantified fluorescence to assess uptake and specificity.

  • Applied nanoparticle conjugation and cell-staining workflows.
  • Analysed EVOS microscopy images using ImageJ and CTCF.
  • Compared fluorescence between lung cancer and normal cell lines.
